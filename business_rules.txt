 UrbanCart Business Rules

 Refund validity rules (a refund is valid only if all are true)
- R1: It has a matching return record.
- R2: The return status is Received.
- R3: The order has a successful payment.
- R4: The refund amount is not more than the amount paid.
- R5: There is only one refund per return.
- R6: The return was requested within 30 days of the order date.

 Exception counting
- Each refund is counted once, using the first rule it fails.
- Priority: R1, R3, R2, R5, R4, R6.
- Value at Risk = full refund amount, except R4, where only the excess over the amount paid is counted.
- Value at Risk is NOT proven loss and NOT a savings estimate. It means "needs investigation".

 Key cleaning rules
- Text fields are trimmed and converted to Proper Case.
- Text dates are read as day-first (DD-MM-YYYY).
- Payment modes are mapped to 5 standard values: UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, COD.
- Invalid order amounts (negative, blank, "N/A") are recalculated as Quantity x Unit Price and flagged.
- Text refund amounts (e.g. "Rs. 2299") are converted to numbers. Negative refund amounts are treated as sign errors and flagged (assumption).
- Exact duplicate rows are removed.
- Orphan records, refunds with no return, and odd dates are flagged, NOT deleted.

 Join rules
- Refunds is the base table.
- Left Outer joins to Returns (Return_ID), Orders (Order_ID) and Payments (Order_ID).
- Row count and refund total must be unchanged after the joins.

 Assumptions and limitations
- The data is synthetic (dummy data).
- The 30-day return window is an assumed policy.
- City and approver samples are small, so differences are patterns to investigate, not proven trends.
